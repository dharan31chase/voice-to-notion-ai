## Part 1: Jobs-to-be-Done Analysis

### What Customers Are Actually Hiring AMS Analytics to Do

#### Job #1: "Help Me Look Good to My Board" ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Job Statement:** *When preparing for board meetings, I want comparative performance data that shows our organization favorably, so I can maintain board confidence and avoid scrutiny.*

**Performance:** Strong painkiller - solves acute anxiety

- Quarterly usage (4x/year per user)  
- High emotional value (reputation protection)  
- **Friction:** Annual data only, requires manual entry

**Segment:** All three segments use this, but Segment A (passionate users) rely on it most

---

#### Job #2: "Help Me Negotiate My Contract/Salary" ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Job Statement:** *When negotiating employment contracts or justifying key hires, I want objective salary benchmarking from comparable organizations, so I can secure fair compensation without appearing uninformed.*

**Performance:** Critical painkiller - solves high-stakes problem

- Infrequent but extremely high-value (every 2-4 years)  
- No good alternatives (consultants expensive, networks incomplete)  
- **Friction:** Annual updates only, data accuracy concerns

**Segment:** Primarily Segment A, occasionally Segment B

---

#### Job #3: "Help Me Budget Realistically" ⭐⭐⭐

**Job Statement:** *When creating annual budgets, I want multi-year trend analysis compared to peers, so I can make informed projections and defend my numbers.*

**Performance:** Table stakes - nice to have, not must-have

- Annual usage (1x/year)  
- Excel is viable alternative (though less elegant)  
- **Friction:** Data entered in annual batches, not integrated with operations

**Segment:** Segment B primarily, some Segment A

---

#### Job #4: "Help Me Stay in the CEO Roundtable" ⭐⭐

**Job Statement:** *When roundtable membership requires AMS Analytics, I need to maintain subscription to access peer learning and networking.*

**Performance:** Compliance requirement - tool itself has low value

- ~25 of 56-59 customers hired primarily for this job  
- **Dependency Risk:** If roundtable unbundles from analytics, these customers churn immediately

**Segment:** Segment C (compliance-driven) exclusively

---

#### Job #5: "Help Me Make Better Operational Decisions" ⭐ (UNFULFILLED)

**Job Statement:** *When facing daily operational challenges, I want real-time insights and recommendations throughout the year, not just during budget season.*

**Performance:** Product doesn't fulfill this job

- Would require real-time data integration  
- Cultural resistance to data-driven operations in performing arts  
- **Strategic Implication:** Don't invest here - market isn't ready

---

## Part 3: Strategic Recommendation - Radical Cost Reduction

### Objective

Achieve 30% operating margin ($135K+ profit) within 6 months by freezing feature development and moving to maintenance-only operations.

This positions the business for sustainable profitability regardless of channel partner fee recurrence, while preserving the core value proposition that passionate users rely on.

---

### Month 1: Immediate Actions

#### 1. Freeze All Feature Development

**Cancel all roadmap items immediately:**

- Demographics data visualization  
- Org chart display/sharing  
- Custom cohorts for compensation  
- P&L user-defined rules  
- All "planned" and "in progress" features  

**Communication to Jordan:** "No new features. Platform stability and data quality only."

**Rationale:** The business cannot afford $370K/year in platform costs while losing money. Feature development must pause until profitability is restored.
