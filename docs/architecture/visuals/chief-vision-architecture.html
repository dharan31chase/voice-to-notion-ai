Three Operating Modes

Different interactions for different contexts

🎙️ "Chief"
Morning Briefing
Start-of-day synthesis. Chief reviews everything and surfaces what matters.
  • 📊 Signal-heavy summary of the day
  • 🚧 Blockers for Deep Work & Family blocks
  • ❓ Decisions needed to unblock goals
  • 🛒 Instacart order ready—confirm? Confirm
  • 📅 Schedule dinner with Jessica Auto
🎙️ "Catch me up"
Deep Work Session
Project-focused collaboration. Chief loads context and works alongside you.
  • 📂 Load project from Strategy Board
  • 🔍 RAG search for relevant files
  • 💭 Deliberate on decisions together Collab
  • ⚠️ Surface conflicting past decisions
  • 💾 Auto-capture progress & update roadmap Auto
🎙️ "Communications block"
Communications Block
Batch processing of email, messages. Chief triages, drafts, sends with approval.
  • 📧 Triage 25 emails into decisions
  • ✍️ Draft responses for batch approval Confirm
  • 📜 Complex items: term sheets, contracts Collab
  • 👨‍⚖️ Schedule lawyer if needed Auto
  • 📤 Send approved emails Auto
